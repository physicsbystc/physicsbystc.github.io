Ampere-Maxwell Law & Displacement Current

Resolution of Ampere's circuital inconsistency between capacitor plates under alternating charge.

INTEGRAL FORMULATION
∮ B ⋅ dl = μ0 ( Ic + ε0 · dΦE/dt )
Where Id = ε0 (dΦE/dt) satisfies continuity of current in dielectric gaps.
check_circle Gauss flux through plate: ΦE = q / ε0
check_circle Differentiating w.r.t time: dΦE/dt = (1/ε0)(dq/dt)
verified Hence: Id = ε0(dΦE/dt) = Ic
